Changelog
=========
A list of changes in the public releases of Forms Filler

Version 0.2.0
-------------

1. Fill out ATF Form 5320.20 (Application to Transport Interstate) for individuals and trusts.
   1. Optional `interstate` settings and `user-data.county` for answers that rarely change
   2. Long answers in narrow fields are printed in a smaller font to fit, and asked for again if they still won't
   3. Trip dates can't start before today or end before they start
2. The 5320.23's caliber or gauge must be a whole number, like `556` or `300`, the same as on the 5320.20.

Version 0.1.0
-------------

1. Initial release.
   1. Fill out ATF Form 5320.23 (Responsible Persons Questionnaire) as separate ATF and CLEO copies
